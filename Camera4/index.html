Promo Reality Check

1) Summary

  • Headline detected: “McVALUE — Buy One, Add One for $1”
  • Offer type: BOGO variant (second item fixed price)
  • Verdict: Fair Value if second item is same or lower price than the first; less effective if you needed only one item.

2) Potential False-Marketing Flags

  • HIGH Ambiguous scope: Poster doesn’t list eligible items or sizes explicitly (only images).
  • MED Price bubble partially obscured: The “$1” appears inside a circle; taxes and regional pricing aren’t mentioned.
  • MED Participation caveat not printed: Typically “at participating locations” applies but isn’t shown.
  • LOW “Value” branding: The “McVALUE” label primes savings even when real savings depend on your item choices.

3) Historical Price Context (mock data)

Twelve months of simulated average menu prices per item to help eyeball whether $1 add-on looks compelling. (Data is generated in JS for demo.)

4) Recommendation

If your second item’s regular price is higher than $1 (it almost always is), the effective discount equals (regular price of cheaper item − $1). For example, pairing a $5.99 chicken sandwich with a $4.79 nuggets set makes the second item ~79% off. If you only want one item, skip the deal and just pay regular price.
